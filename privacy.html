Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

App

This Privacy Policy applies to Calculator.

Overview

Calculator is a utility app for basic calculations, scientific calculations, and unit conversions. The app does not require an account and does not display ads.

Personal Data

Calculator does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, files, microphone data, camera data, identifiers, analytics data, advertising data, or payment data.

Local Data Stored on Your Device

Calculator may store calculation history, app settings, converter order, and cached currency exchange rates locally on your device.

This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. You can clear calculation history inside the app. You can also remove local app data by clearing the app storage in your Android system settings.

Internet Access

Calculator uses internet access only to fetch currency exchange-rate information.

When using currency conversion, the app may request public exchange-rate data from an exchange-rate service over HTTPS. The request may include currency codes such as USD or TWD. The app does not send your entered amount, calculation history, or personal data.

If the network is unavailable, Calculator may use the last cached exchange rate stored locally on your device.

Voice, Sound, and Haptic Feedback

Calculator includes optional voice feedback, keyboard sound, and haptic feedback.

Voice feedback uses the device's built-in speech synthesis feature. The app does not record your voice and does not use the microphone.

Haptic feedback uses the device vibration feature.

Analytics and Advertising

Calculator does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or third-party advertising networks.

Children

Calculator is a general utility app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. Updates will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
