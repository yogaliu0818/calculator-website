Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions. No account required.

Does it work offline?

Core calculations and unit conversions work offline. New exchange rates need internet; previously cached rates can be reused. Speech depends on installed device voices.

Can I use it on my tablet?

Yes. Phones and tablets are supported in portrait and landscape, on Android 7.0 or later.

Where are my calculations stored?

History, favorites and settings are saved locally in the app. There is no account-based cloud sync. See the privacy policy for details.
